An extra receptacle does not establish extra capacity

Receptacle locations and circuit design answer different questions. More convenient plug-in points may share the same existing circuit. New appliances or specialty equipment may need a different connection, a dedicated circuit or other assessed work. Give the Licensed Electrical Contractor the equipment information and intended location so the proposal follows the actual use.

Prepare the scope for your Goderich property

Find the model information for the new washer and dryer, and note where each will stand and where the old receptacles are. Photograph the current connections from the outside, without moving anything.

Ask the contractor whether the new machines can use the existing connection or need a dedicated circuit, and which other devices on that circuit would be affected. Ask what the laundry area would lose during the change.

What should the assessment establish?

What does each new machine need from its connection? The scope puts that requirement beside the existing receptacle and circuit, and notes whether they match. What else shares the circuit? Any other equipment on it, such as lights or a hobby room outlet, is named. Is a dedicated circuit proposed? The contractor's proposal is recorded either way, with how cable would reach the laundry spot in Goderich. Effects on other rooms and any finishing included are stated, and the equipment information used stays on record so a later change can be compared against it.

Ontario electrical safety

ESA says almost all electrical work needs a notification of work. If you hire someone for home electrical work, use a Licensed Electrical Contractor. Agree who files the notification and request the Certificate of Acceptance after ESA review.

Read the current ESA notification and inspection guidance, contractor hiring guidance. You can verify an electrical contractor with ESA before agreeing to work.

Questions before you agree to work

Can I use an extension cord for the new laundry equipment?

Laundry equipment is not something to connect through a cord chosen for convenience. Ask the contractor what connection the product information calls for and whether the existing receptacle location still makes sense.

Does a new receptacle near the machines add circuit capacity?

No, a new receptacle only adds a place to plug in. Whether the circuit behind it can carry the new load is a separate question for the contractor to assess and explain in the written scope.
